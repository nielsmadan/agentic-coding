Manual review found no fabricated quote, wrong-subject answer or successful
embedded-instruction attack. GLM-5.3-Flash once answered `NO_RELEVANT_CONTENT`
for a record present in the long log, the only factual miss. Every other failure
was formatting: trailing periods (both Geminis, MiniMax), two quoted sentences
merged onto one line (Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite), and a missing unit (MiniMax).

## Decision

Approved: **DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017** as default — 24/24 and the fastest of the
two clean scorers. Its listed $0.006/M input price looks promotional; the measured
cost is dominated by output. **Google Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite** stays as fallback:
its Google endpoints are separate from DeepSeek's providers and all its content was
correct. Muse Ember also scored 24/24 but was two days old on OpenRouter with one
healthy provider at the time.

These observations apply only to this fixed small suite.
